# Sushaasan: Outreach Playbook for an AI-Governance Founder in India (May 2026)

## TL;DR
- **Skip "civic-tech communities" theater and go where DPI is actually built**: the highest-leverage moves for a pre-launch, DPIIT-recognized AI-governance founder are (a) applying to the **iSPIRT volunteer pathway** (the de-facto India Stack network gated by Sharad Sharma), (b) onboarding to **Sahamati's Account Aggregator** and **ONDC** participant communities, and (c) building a public posting cadence on X that tags a tight cluster of ~10 named bureaucrats and DPI architects — not generic "policy influencers."
- **The most leverage-rich programs to target in the next 6–12 months** are the **India Smart Cities Fellowship** (MoHUA/NIUA, ₹60K/month, embeds you with a city), **AIM-PRIME** (NITI Aayog + Venture Center + Gates Foundation for deep-tech founders), the **IndiaAI Innovation Challenge 2026** (MeitY pilot with line ministries), **iDEX Open Challenge** (₹1.5 crore SPARK grants, deadline 30 Jun 2026), and the **Maharashtra CM Fellowship** (application opens 8 May 2026, places you with a District Collector). Most one-shot "youth policy fellowships" (YLAC PiA, LAMP, Mukherjee) are useful for credibility but won't move a product forward.
- **Tag, don't pitch.** The shortest path to government attention is repeatable, sourced public commentary on AI-governance edge cases (DPDP rules, Section 69A takedowns, AI in welfare delivery) tagging Abhishek Singh (CEO IndiaAI Mission), Jayesh Ranjan (Telangana IT), Sanjeev Sanyal (PM-EAC), Debjani Ghosh (NITI Frontier Tech Hub) and Sharad Sharma — over 4–6 weeks, not a one-off cold DM.

## Key Findings

1. **There is no large open Discord/Telegram for Indian GovTech.** The ecosystem is deliberately gated through volunteer cohorts (iSPIRT), builder Slacks behind onboarding (Sahamati AA, ONDC), and invite-only conclaves (Agami, Carnegie India Global Technology Summit). Pre-launch founders should not waste time hunting for a public chatroom — they should apply to the gatekeepers and ship publicly on X/LinkedIn.

2. **The IAS Twitterati split into three groups, only one of which matters for you.** "Mass-reach IAS influencers" (Awanish Sharan 619K per his @AwanishSharan profile bio, Sonal Goel ~528K) drive citizen-side virality but rarely engage on technical policy. "Operating bureaucrats" (Jayesh Ranjan, Abhishek Singh, Saurabh Garg, R.S. Sharma) actually run AI/DPI programs — these are the ones to engage substantively. "Ex-government policy voices" (Sanjeev Sanyal, Amitabh Kant, Rajeev Chandrasekhar, Debjani Ghosh) shape narrative and frequently amplify young Indian founders.

3. **Several flagship programs have already closed for 2026** — SBI Youth for India (closed 30 Apr 2026), Digital India Internship summer cycle (closed 27 Apr), Youth Co:Lab 2026 (closed 20 Mar). Plan for 2027 cycles. Programs that **are open or open continuously**: NITI Aayog Internship (1–10th of every month), Maharashtra CM Fellowship (8 May 2026 application open), Startup India / DPIIT 80-IAC tax-exemption, IndiaAI Innovation Challenge, iDEX Open Challenge (deadline 30 Jun 2026), Civis Policy Leaders Internship (rolling), ISPP PDM (rolling, ~June final cut-off).

4. **Your DPIIT recognition + Bhaskar ID are entry tickets, not differentiators.** As of 31 January 2026, 2,12,283 startups have been DPIIT-recognized per PIB Lok Sabha press release PRID 2241313 ("a total of 2,12,283 entities have been recognised as startups by the Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade"). What differentiates you for a bureaucrat is a working pilot, a deployment with even one ULB (urban local body) or Gram Panchayat, or a public artifact (research paper, open-source repo) on AI safety in governance. Lead every outreach with that artifact, not the company.

5. **The single biggest opportunity in India's AI-governance space right now is the IndiaAI Mission's compute and dataset platform** — 34,000 GPUs available at ~Rs 115–150/hour (~42% below market), scaling to 100,000 by end of 2026, under a ₹10,371.92 crore programme approved by Union Cabinet on 7 March 2024 (PIB press release PRID 2012355: "The Cabinet chaired by the Prime Minister Shri Narendra Modi has approved the comprehensive national-level IndiaAI mission with a budget outlay of Rs.10,371.92 crore"). As a DPIIT-recognized AI-governance startup you are a primary target audience for subsidised compute and the IndiaAI Application Development Initiative (IADI). Apply.

### Days 60–90: Convert to government access
- File for **Section 80-IAC** tax exemption — separate from DPIIT recognition; required for serious credibility with government buyers and angels.
- Make one **first pilot proposal in writing** to a single ULB or Smart City SPV via the smartnet.niua.org channel, leveraging the artifact you published in Day 14. One pilot beats ten policy papers.
- File an **op-ed pitch** to The Print, Hindu BusinessLine, or Carnegie India commentary inbox on a Sushaasan-relevant policy question (e.g., "Why India's AI-governance stack should be procurement-first, not regulation-first"). Use the byline "Founder, Sushaasan; DPIIT-recognized startup."

### Benchmarks that should change your plan
- If you are not into **iSPIRT Balloon Volunteering** by Day 90, escalate via warm intros from a Takshashila alum or directly via Sanjay Jain / Siddharth Shetty.
- If your IndiaAI / iDEX applications do not produce a webinar invite or shortlist, pivot to **Telangana T-AIM** (via Jayesh Ranjan's office) or **Karnataka Wadhwani Foundation State Capability Enhancement Program** — state governments move 3–5× faster than the centre for first pilots.

## Caveats

- **Many "fellowships" are about your career, not your startup.** PMRDF, Gandhi Fellowship, SBI YFI, LAMP, Mukherjee are valuable but consume 1–2 years of your time. For a solo founder pre-launch, they are usually the wrong move; pick a part-time policy credential (Takshashila GCPP) and a startup-grant track (TIDE 2.0 / SISFS / IndiaAI) instead.
- **DPIIT recognition's value is not access; it's compliance.** It unlocks 80-IAC and grant eligibility, but does not by itself open ministry doors. Treat it as table stakes — especially given that as of 31 January 2026, the PIB press release PRID 2241313 confirms "2,12,283 entities have been recognised as startups by the Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT)."
- **The IndiaAI Mission is in heavy build-out** — ₹10,371.92 crore approved by Union Cabinet on 7 March 2024 per PIB press release PRID 2012355 ("The Cabinet chaired by the Prime Minister Shri Narendra Modi has approved the comprehensive national-level IndiaAI mission with a budget outlay of Rs.10,371.92 crore") — but is largely procurement-driven; expect cycles of 4–9 months from challenge to pilot. Apply early, iterate publicly.
- **Watch the DPDP Rules timeline closely** — notified 13/14 November 2025; per the Grant Thornton DPDP Rules 2025 brochure, "Phase II – 13 November 2026 (12 months from notification)" opens Consent Manager registration; per Lexology's analysis, "These provisions will become effective 18 months from the date of publication of the Enforcement Notification in the official gazette," placing Phase 3 full obligations on 14 May 2027. Sushaasan's positioning will depend on whether it is classified as a Significant Data Fiduciary.
- **Source-quality flag**: Follower counts and program timelines are as of May 2026; verify each link before applying. Several program pages list opening dates that have historically slipped 4–8 weeks. The Maharashtra CM Fellowship's 8 May 2026 application date is the date I have the highest confidence on; verify directly at mahades.maharashtra.gov.in/FELLOWSHIP/.
- **One important uncertainty**: AIM-PRIME and ANIC have not announced fresh 2026 call timelines as of this report; the AIM 2.0 budget (₹2,750 cr through Mar 2028) has been approved, but individual program calendars are episodic. Set up alerts on aim.gov.in.
