## THE MATH OF COLD OUTREACH

If you contact 50 investors this week:
- 30% will open your message (~15)
- 10% will respond (~5)
- 5% will take a call (~2-3)
- 1-2% will show serious interest (~1)

That means you need to contact 50+ to get 1 serious conversation. Contact 100+ to get 2-3 serious conversations. Contact 200+ to create competitive urgency.

**Volume is your friend. Every "no" gets you closer to "yes." Every ignored DM is practice for the one that lands.**
