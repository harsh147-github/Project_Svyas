## The civic tech market is ready — here's the investment context

India's civic tech ecosystem has **450-475 startups** with over **$100M invested** to date. The institutional anchor is **Omidyar Network India**, the only major investor with an explicit "Governance & Citizen Engagement" mandate — they co-launched the Citizen Innovation Lab with CIIE.CO at IIM Ahmedabad. **Indian Angel Network's COO has publicly endorsed civic tech startups.** The government's Digital India push, Smart Cities Mission, and DPDP Bill create regulatory tailwinds.

Typical pre-seed check sizes in India range from **₹10 lakh to ₹50 lakh per angel**, with syndicated rounds through networks reaching ₹1-5 crore collectively. Standard pre-seed rounds target ₹1-5 crore at ₹2-5 crore pre-money valuation, diluting 10-20% equity. Angel tax was **fully abolished in April 2025**, removing a major friction point.

Sushaasan's positioning should lean on three narratives simultaneously: (1) "Blinkit of civic participation" for aggregation-model investors like Deepinder Goyal, Anupam Mittal, and Ritesh Agarwal; (2) "The civic layer of India Stack" for DPI ecosystem players like Sharad Sharma and Nandan Nilekani; and (3) "Delta 4 for governance" for thesis-driven investors like Kunal Shah.
