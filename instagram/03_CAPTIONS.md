## POST 4 · HOW IT WORKS — Day 4

> Chaos in. Clarity out. In four steps. Built in Pune by one person.

**Step 01 · Listen.**
Public Instagram, Twitter, Reddit, Telegram — scanned daily. Reviews and news every hour. 22-language NLP. PII stripped before storage. Deduplicated. Geo-tagged to your ward. No login. No personal data collected. Closed groups off-limits.

**Step 02 · Find the pattern.**
Claude Sonnet classifies every post — issue type, severity 1–5, location, sentiment. Voyage-3 multilingual embeddings cluster similar voices in the same ward. 10,000 individual reports merge into one structured signal.

**Step 03 · Synthesize the solution.**
Claude Opus drafts the intervention: step-by-step plan, responsible department, cost estimate in ₹, timeline in days. Every claim traces to a real cited post. No invention. No vague "coordinate with PMC". Concrete actions only.

**Step 04 · Show both sides.**
Government dashboard: priority queue, named officer, cost, timeline. Citizen dashboard: plain language, status badges, resolution date. When the corporator marks an issue resolved — citizens see it on the same screen, instantly.

The loop closes publicly. That is the whole point.

Stack: Next.js · Inngest · Supabase · Anthropic Claude (Sonnet + Opus) · Apify · Perplexity · Voyage-3 · Mapbox.
