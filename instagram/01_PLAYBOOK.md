## 1. What Namma Kasa actually did right (and why we copy the pattern)

Jyothish V M's NammaKasa wasn't viral by accident. It hit five levers in sequence:

| Lever | What he did | Why it worked |
|---|---|---|
| **Founder-as-protagonist** | One named human ("Bengaluru product designer Jyothish, 31") | Press has a face. Citizens trust a person, not a brand. |
| **Shipped before announcing** | App live. Map populated. Real complaints visible. | Demo > promise. Algorithm and humans both reward proof. |
| **One-line product hook** | "Fix your city in 30 seconds." | Repeatable. Quotable. Headline-ready. |
| **Privacy as a feature** | "No login. No personal data." | Removes the #1 friction citizens have with gov apps. |
| **Accountability twist** | MLA/MP leaderboard | Gave news outlets a "stakes" angle. *The Better India* picked it up day 3. |

**Within 4 days:** 230 reports, 45,000 users. Press came to *him*.

**Sushaasan's parallel:**
- Founder-as-protagonist → **the team, solo founder, Pune**
- Shipped before announcing → **NIBM pilot is LIVE. 19k signals. 4 ranked interventions.**
- One-line hook → **"The signal layer for Indian governance."** (Backup: *"Chaos in. Clarity out."*)
- Privacy as a feature → **"No login. No personal data. We listen to what you already say."**
- Accountability twist → **Ward dashboard. Corporator named. Loop closed publicly.**

We already have all five levers. We just have to land them in order.

## 2. Anatomy of a winning civic-tech post (2026 Instagram algorithm)

Carousel is the format. Algorithm in May 2026 weights *dwell time, completion rate, saves, shares-to-DM* far above likes. A carousel is multiple swipes = multiple micro-commitments = max signal.

**Slide count:** 7–10 slides. Sweet spot.
**Aspect ratio:** 1080 × 1350 (portrait 4:5).
**First slide rules:**
- Hook in ≤ 10 words.
- Answers *"is this for me?"* AND *"what will I get if I swipe?"*
- Big type. Black or navy background. Saffron or green accent.
- Visible "→ swipe" cue on slide 1 only.

**Story arc (always):**
1. Hook
2. Problem stat (a single number, large)
3. Why old solutions fail
4. The new mechanism (in one sentence)
5. Proof — screenshot or pilot number
6. What it means for the citizen
7. CTA (follow + DM + share)

**Save/share triggers (use 2 per carousel):**
- "Save this if you've ever been ignored by a complaint portal."
- "Send this to the person in your housing society WhatsApp who keeps complaining."
- "Tag your ward corporator if you want them to see this."

## 3. Visual rules (non-negotiable, matches CLAUDE.md brand)

| Element | Spec |
|---|---|
| Background | `#0A0A0A` (graphite) or `#0B1F3A` (deep navy) |
| Primary accent | `#FF9933` (saffron) |
| Secondary accent | `#138808` (India green) |
| Text on dark | `#FFFFFF` |
| Header font | Source Serif 4 (or Cormorant Garamond fallback) |
| Body font | Inter |
| Logo mark | Devanagari **स** in saffron, top-left, always |
| Motion (Reels) | Scroll/reveal only. No floating, no idle loops. |
| Chart palette | Saffron → navy gradient. Never rainbow. |

**Hard nos:**
- No emoji-soup captions.
- No startup-purple gradients.
- No anti-government framing. Ever. We help **both** sides.
- No "complaint", "protest", "petition" language. We say **"signal"** and **"solution"**.

## 4. Caption formula

```
[ HOOK — 1 line, can stand alone if no one swipes ]

[ PROBLEM — 2 lines max, one stat ]

[ SOLUTION — 2 lines, what Sushaasan does, in plain Hindi-friendly English ]

[ PROOF — 1 line, real pilot data ]

[ CTA — follow / DM / share to ward WhatsApp ]

—
@sushaasan.in
sushaasan.in

#Sushaasan #Pune #CivicTech #GovTech #IndianGovernance #DigitalIndia #PMC #SmartCity #NIBM #Wanowrie #Kondhwa #AIforIndia #PuneTraffic #BengaluruTraffic #AfterUPI #SovereignAI
```

Use **12–18 hashtags max**. Mix three buckets: broad (#DigitalIndia), niche (#PuneTraffic), geo (#NIBM). Rotate so Instagram doesn't flag as spam.

## 5. The 5-post launch series

| Day | Post | Hook | Job |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Manifesto** | "After Aadhaar. After UPI. This is next." | Plant the flag. Define the category. |
| 2 | **The problem** | "500 million Indians are talking. Nobody is listening." | Make the gap visceral. |
| 3 | **The demo (NIBM live)** | "We just decoded one Pune traffic jam. Here's the receipt." | Prove it works. |
| 4 | **How it works** | "Chaos in. Clarity out. In four steps." | Educate. Build credibility. |
| 5 | **Founder story + CTA** | "I'm the team. I'm 22. I built this alone." | Trigger press + DMs. |

Post **one per day at 8:30 PM IST** (peak Pune scroll window). Stories every day amplifying the live carousel.

## 6. After day 5

- **Day 6:** Reel — screen recording of sushaasan.in/dashboard/NIBM with voiceover.
- **Day 7:** Carousel — "5 things citizens told us this week that PMC didn't know."
- **Day 8 onward:** Weekly cadence. One signal → solution case study per week. This is the Namma Kasa flywheel.

## 7. Outreach with the launch (do these the same day post #1 goes live)

1. DM *The Better India*, *YourStory*, *Inc42*, *MoneyControl*, *Mint Lounge* — one-line pitch + link to post #3 (the demo). They eat civic-tech stories.
2. Tag PMC, Pune Police, Pune Mayor's office (with respect — we are not against them).
3. Cross-post to LinkedIn (same captions, slightly more formal).
4. Send to 10 Pune ward WhatsApp groups with the line *"Built this for our ward. No login, no data collection. Feedback welcome."*

## 8. What success looks like in 7 days

- 500+ followers (Namma Kasa hit similar in 3 days; we have a stronger product narrative)
- 1 press pickup (Better India / YourStory / local Pune outlet)
- 50+ DMs from citizens about their ward
- 1 corporator or PMC official reaches out

That's the trigger to move to weekly case-study cadence and start the gov outreach in earnest.
