# Sushaasan Launch Storyboard

## Scene 1 — Hook

- Duration: `0-10s`
- Screen:
  Hero section of the dashboard.
- Voiceover:
  "India mein opinions ki kami nahi hai..."
- On-screen emphasis:
  `Sushaasan is not a grievance dashboard.`

## Scene 2 — Scattered-Source Montage

- Duration: `10-25s`
- Screen:
  Evidence panel cards.
- Capture:
  Instagram reel, local news, GMaps review, resident forum, manual note, traffic reading.
- Visual purpose:
  Make the USP explicit: the raw material is scattered across the internet.
- Voiceover beat:
  "Reels, tweets, reviews, local news, WhatsApp discussions..."

## Scene 3 — NIBM Digital Twin

- Duration: `25-38s`
- Screen:
  Live Ops Map.
- Capture:
  Move across hotspots, show active cluster changing.
- Visual purpose:
  NIBM feels like a living governance system, not a static map.
- Voiceover beat:
  "Yeh NIBM traffic management region ka demo hai..."

## Scene 4 — Root Cause Reveal

- Duration: `38-50s`
- Screen:
  Cluster cards + causal chain cards.
- Capture:
  Highlight illegal parking -> lane narrowing -> manual weaving -> corridor spillback.
- Visual purpose:
  Show that Sushaasan finds small causes behind the visible big problem.
- Voiceover beat:
  "Problem sirf traffic nahi hai..."

## Scene 5 — Optimized Action Layer

- Duration: `50-70s`
- Screen:
  Intervention stack and primary strategy card.
- Capture:
  Ranked interventions, authority ownership, expected impact, citizen role.
- Visual purpose:
  Prove output is a coordinated solution, not a complaint summary.
- Voiceover beat:
  "Matlab sirf problem nahi, best executable solution..."

## Scene 6 — Routing + Citizen Role

- Duration: `70-82s`
- Screen:
  Routing ladder and citizen role cards.
- Capture:
  Show ward -> traffic police -> municipal -> city progression.
- Visual purpose:
  Make procedure-aware execution legible.
- Voiceover beat:
  "Kis governance level par action lena chahiye, aur citizens ka role kya hoga."

## Scene 7 — Scale-Out + CTA

- Duration: `82-90s`
- Screen:
  Footer CTA section.
- Visual purpose:
  Transition from NIBM proof case to national ambition.
- Voiceover beat:
  "Today NIBM. Kal har ward, har city, har state."

## Editing Notes

- Use fast cuts for Scene 2.
- Use slower, more confident pans for the map and interventions.
- Keep subtitles always on.
- Favor punchy captions:
  - `Scattered signals`
  - `Root causes`
  - `Ranked action`
  - `Citizen + state execution`
  - `Today NIBM. Tomorrow India.`
