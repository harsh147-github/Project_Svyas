## Reddit Version

I’ve been building a startup idea called **Sushaasan** and wanted to share the first demo.

The idea:

India already produces huge amounts of civic signal online, but it’s scattered across comments, reels, reviews, local news, public discussions, and social posts.

Sushaasan tries to turn that scattered public reality into something useful:

- not a grievance app
- not a complaint dashboard
- but a governance intelligence engine

The first demo is focused on the NIBM traffic management region in Pune.

The goal is to show that a traffic problem is usually not just traffic. It’s a combination of parking, encroachment, construction spillover, enforcement gaps, pedestrian failure, and civic behavior.

If those scattered signals can be synthesized well, better civic action can come out of them.

Would love honest feedback from builders, policy people, civic-tech folks, and anyone who thinks this is interesting.
